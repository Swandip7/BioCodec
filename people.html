Swandip Singha is an undergraduate researcher in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) and the founder of BioCodec Lab. His research interests lie at the intersection of physiological signal processing and intelligent computing, with a focus on robust, interpretable, and reproducible methods for biomedical sensing.
