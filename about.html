About

BioCodec Lab is an independent research initiative focused on developing reliable computational approaches for biomedical sensing.

We are interested in the intersection of signal processing and intelligent computing, with an emphasis on methods that are robust, interpretable, and reproducible. Our work explores how computational models can move beyond benchmark performance toward reliable inference under real-world measurement conditions.

We aim to contribute open research and develop methods that are both technically rigorous and meaningful for practical biomedical applications.
